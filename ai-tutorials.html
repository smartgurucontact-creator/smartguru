🤖 AI Tutorials

Learn Artificial Intelligence and AI tools step by step in simple language.

1. AI kya hai?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) computer technology hai jo information ko samajhne, patterns identify karne aur useful responses dene jaise kaam kar sakti hai.

2. ChatGPT ka use

ChatGPT se questions pooch sakte hain, ideas le sakte hain, writing mein help aur difficult topics ko simple language mein samajh sakte hain.

3. Gemini ka use

Gemini ek AI assistant hai jo questions answer karne, learning, writing aur creative ideas mein madad kar sakta hai.

4. AI Image Generation

AI image tools mein text prompt likhkar naye images create kiye ja sakte hain.

Example prompt: A beautiful village at sunrise, green fields, cinematic lighting, realistic photography.

5. AI Video Generation

Kuch AI tools text prompts ya images se short videos generate kar sakte hain. Video banane ke liye scene, characters aur movement clearly describe karo.

6. Prompt writing

AI ko clear instructions dena prompt writing kehlata hai.

Example: Explain HTML in simple Hindi with an easy example for a beginner.
