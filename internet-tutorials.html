🌐 Internet Tutorials

Learn internet basics, websites, browsers and online safety in simple language.

1. Internet kya hai?

Internet duniya bhar ke computers aur devices ko connect karne wala network hai. Isse information share aur communicate kar sakte hain.

2. Browser kya hai?

Browser ek app hai jisse websites open karte hain. Chrome, Firefox aur Edge popular browsers hain.

3. Website kya hai?

Website internet par available pages ka collection hoti hai. Websites information, education, entertainment aur services provide karti hain.

4. Search engine ka use

Google jaise search engines par keywords likhkar information, websites aur answers search kar sakte hain.

5. Online safety

  • OTP aur passwords kisi ko share mat karo.
  • Unknown links par click karne se pehle check karo.
  • Strong passwords use karo.
  • Personal information unknown websites par mat do.

6. HTTPS kya hai?

HTTPS website aur browser ke beech data connection ko encrypt karne mein madad karta hai. Lekin HTTPS hone ka matlab ye nahi ki website automatically trustworthy hai.
