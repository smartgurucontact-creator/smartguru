📱 Android Phone Tips

Learn useful Android tips and tricks in simple language.

1. Phone Storage Check

Open Settings → Storage to check how much storage is used and available.

2. Battery Usage

Open Settings → Battery → Battery Usage to see which apps consume battery.

3. App Permissions

Open Settings → Privacy → Permission Manager. Review which apps have access to your camera, microphone and location.

4. Keep Your Phone Updated

Open Settings → System Update and check whether an official update is available.

5. Protect Your Phone

Use a screen lock, never share OTPs, and install apps from trusted sources.
